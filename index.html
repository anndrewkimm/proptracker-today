How to read this

Picks for today's NBA games that haven't started, using DraftKings' lines. Check the PrizePicks line before you pick. Chance is DraftKings' odds with their cut removed, adjusted for Less vs More and his last 10 games. Stars count two signs that have beaten the odds, on new games too: a Less pick, and past the line in 6+ of his last 10. Standard means close to a coin flip, like a regular PrizePicks line. Goblin-like is an easy More that would pay less. Demon-like is an easy Less at a line PrizePicks would make a demon, which is More only, so it probably isn't offered. Break-even on PrizePicks is about 54% a pick (5-6 Flex) to 58% (2-pick Power).

Connection help

Refresh retries the data feeds. If a connection fails, a recent saved board can be shown with its age. Saved picks expire after 30 minutes; games disappear at tip-off.

Open this page once while connected so this phone can keep a copy for temporary outages. Offline access depends on your browser keeping its saved data.
