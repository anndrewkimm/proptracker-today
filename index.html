How to read this

Picks for today's NBA games that haven't started, using DraftKings' lines. Check the PrizePicks line before you pick. Chance follows DraftKings' current prices with their cut removed. There is no automatic Under or old-hit-rate bonus. Stars describe evidence: recent history/report coverage, and no detected role or line mismatch. They do not mean an extra betting edge. Why this pick compares the line with minutes scenarios; absences trigger review, not an automatic Over. Standard means close to a coin flip, like a regular PrizePicks line. Goblin-like is an easy More that would pay less. Demon-like is an easy Less at a line PrizePicks would make a demon, which is More only, so it probably isn't offered. Break-even on PrizePicks is about 54% a pick (5-6 Flex) to 58% (2-pick Power).

Connection help

Refresh retries the data feeds. If a connection fails, a recent saved board can be shown with its age. Saved picks expire after 30 minutes; games disappear at tip-off.

Open this page once while connected so this phone can keep a copy for temporary outages. Offline access depends on your browser keeping its saved data.
